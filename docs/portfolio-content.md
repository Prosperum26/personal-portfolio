# About Me

## Introduction

- I have been passionate about technology since childhood, which led me to pursue a degree in Information Systems. Initially, I was fascinated by understanding how complex systems are designed and how different components work together.

- During my university years, I had the opportunity to participate in an AI research project under the guidance of one of my professors. That experience sparked a deeper interest in artificial intelligence and inspired me to explore the field further.

- At this stage, I focus on web development because it provides a strong engineering foundation and valuable experience in building real-world software. At the same time, I continuously expand my knowledge across related areas such as DevOps, security, and AI, with the long-term goal of developing intelligent applications that solve meaningful problems.

## My Story

- My journey into software development began with curiosity rather than a specific career plan. I was always interested in understanding how technology works behind the scenes, which naturally led me to study Information Systems.

- As I explored different areas of software engineering, I realized that every field contributes to building reliable systems. I have spent time learning backend development, frontend development, DevOps, and basic security concepts to gain a broader perspective instead of limiting myself to a single technology stack.

- The turning point came when I joined an AI research project. Since then, my long-term vision has shifted toward combining software engineering with artificial intelligence to build practical, intelligent products.
